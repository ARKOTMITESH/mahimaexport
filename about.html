Corporate Profile

Mahima Global Entrepreneurs

A division of Mahima Global Entrepreneurs OPC Private Limited. Facilitating bilateral global trade as a premier ExIm Corporation: sourcing and exporting premium Indian agricultural products while importing essential industrial materials and machinery to power domestic growth.

Watch Corporate Presentation

Global Standards. Bilateral Trade Excellence.

Mahima Global Entrepreneurs OPC Private Limited was incorporated on **January 10, 2025**, with the mission of establishing a world-class trade bridge. Sourcing agricultural commodities, premium rice, organic vetiver roots, and industrial materials directly from verified farms and mines across India, we oversee the entire export process from inspection to freight delivery.

Our core operational framework emphasizes absolute supply-chain transparency, laboratory testing certifications (such as APEDA, Spices Board, and ISO compliance), and customized container packaging. We work on the foundation that commercial transactions must deliver equal value to both local growers and global buyers.

Export Sourcing Division (Ex-India)

Our export operations facilitate direct farm-to-port supply pipelines of premium Indian commodities to international markets. Operating under official trade licenses issued by the DGFT, APEDA, FSSAI, and the Spices Board of India, we manage the procurement, grading, custom cleaning, and packaging of agricultural exports (premium Basmati and non-Basmati rice, organic ground spices, tea, coffee, fruits, and vegetables) and industrial-grade raw materials (jute, combed cotton yarn, mica flakes).

Compliance & Logistics: End-to-end documentation including Bill of Lading (B/L), Certificate of Origin (COO), Phytosanitary clearances, third-party quality inspections (SGS/Bureau Veritas), and temperature-controlled container logistics.

Import Operations Division (In-India)

We serve as a strategic procurement channel for Indian manufacturing, chemical compounding, printing, and machining industries. We specialize in importing bulk specialty polymers (LLDPE granules, PP homopolymers, PVC suspension resins), industrial chemical solvents (Isopropyl Alcohol, Ethyl Acetate), and advanced precision machinery components (CNC tooling heads, laser modules) directly from certified manufacturers in Saudi Arabia, Germany, Japan, and other origin markets.

Compliance & Custom Clearing: Custom House Agent (CHA) clearance handling at major ports (JNPT, Chennai, Mundra), BIS compliance validation, safety data sheet (SDS/REACH) audits, custom bonded warehouse storage, and last-mile inland freight delivery.

Bilateral Trade Philosophy

Empowering Domestic Industries & Global Buyers

Our trade model is founded on deep, direct partnerships in both directions. For exports, we establish stable supply agreements with agricultural cooperatives and farmers in major producing zones across India, eliminating intermediary brokers to deliver equitable pricing and guaranteed traceability to international buyers.

For imports, we partner with world-class manufacturers of chemical solvents, polymer resins, and high-precision machinery, ensuring local Indian enterprises and infrastructure projects have direct access to premium industrial raw materials with streamlined customs clearance and tariff optimization.

Trade Standards

Our Strict Quality Gateways

Every cargo shipment undergoes a multi-stage physical and chemical inspection process to guarantee international regulatory compliance.

STAGE 01

Pre-Harvest Audit

We verify agricultural chemical use, soil condition, and grain moisture levels prior to crop harvest.

STAGE 02

Sieving & Sorting

Grains and spices undergo mechanical sorting to eliminate debris, black seeds, and size inconsistencies.

STAGE 03

Lab Verification

Certified lab assays check for heavy metals (e.g. Lead chromate), aflatoxins, and pesticide residues.

STAGE 04

Vacuum Packing

Vacuum packaging in multi-layer bags prevents moisture ingress and cargo infestation during maritime transit.
