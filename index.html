WHO WE ARE

Building Long-Term
Global Trade Relationships

Mahima Global Entrepreneurs, a premier trade division of Mahima Global Entrepreneurs OPC Private Limited, acts as a pivotal bilateral conduit for international trade. Founded on January 10, 2025, by our Director, Sunil Kumar Pyna, the corporation manages full-scale bilateral trade operations, ensuring strict regulatory alignment, contract compliance, and top-tier logistics across global corridors.

Our Export Sourcing Division connects certified Indian agricultural producers and mining operations directly to global markets, delivering premium Basmati and non-Basmati rice, organic spices, fresh perishables, tea, coffee, and industrial minerals with complete phytosanitary, APEDA, and ISO clearance. Complementing this, our Import Operations Division facilitates the procurement and custom clearing (CHA clearance, customs bonded storage, and BIS compliance validation) of bulk industrial polymers, chemical solvents, and precision machinery components directly to Indian manufacturers, establishing robust, balanced bilateral trade routes.

Yes. We provide comprehensive, end-to-end export solutions. This includes initial product sourcing, independent quality inspections (SGS/Bureau Veritas), customized packaging, container booking, custom house agency (CHA) clearance at loading ports, phytosanitary/fumigation documentation, Certificate of Origin paperwork, and sea/air freight forwarding to your destination port.

Absolutely. We specialize in private labeling (OEM) and custom branding services. We can customize retail bags, bulk woven sacks, corrugated cardboard master cartons, vacuum packaging, and specialized labeling according to your brand design guidelines, local market language requirements, and country-specific food labelling laws.

We support internationally recognized, secure payment terms to ensure mutual security:
• Telegraphic Transfer (Advance T/T)
• Irrevocable Letter of Credit at Sight (L/C)
• Documents Against Payment (D/P) / Documentary Collection
All payment structures are agreed upon in advance and detailed transparently inside the commercial proforma invoice.

MOQ varies depending on the product category. For staple commodities (like Basmati and Non-Basmati rice, spices), our standard MOQ is 1 Full Container Load (FCL) of either 20ft or 40ft (ranging from 12 MT to 25 MT). For high-value oils, vetiver extracts, and specialty blends, we accept smaller trial quantities. Please consult our trade specialists for specific MOQs.
