California Almonds (Nonpareil)

CODE: IMP-ALM

Premium raw Nonpareil almond kernels from California

Premium grade California Almonds (Nonpareil variety, size 23/25) imported directly from orchards. Selected for their thin shells, smooth kernels, and sweet flavor. Processed in pasteurized, FSSAI-compliant packaging centers in India.

Commercial Advantages

  • ◈ Grade A Nonpareil variety with large, uniform kernel sizes.
  • ◈ Low moisture content, ensuring long shelf life and crunchiness.
  • ◈ Excellent for direct retail, confectionery, or almond milk processing.
  • ◈ Imported in temperature-controlled reefers to prevent oxidation.

Health & Wellness Benefits

  • ◈ Rich in Vitamin E, magnesium, and heart-healthy monounsaturated fats.
  • ◈ Helps manage cholesterol levels and supports vascular health.
  • ◈ High plant protein and dietary fiber content for satiety.
  • ◈ Naturally low glycemic load, great for diabetic snack foods.
