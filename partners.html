The Pillars of Our Sourcing Ecosystem

Agricultural Cooperatives

We maintain direct contract farming agreements with cooperatives representing 10,000+ hectares across India. This direct link eliminates intermediaries, ensuring premium quality at origin while paying local farmers +25% above market baseline.

Workflow Integration

Our Partner Onboarding Journey

01

Initial Consultation & Compliance Review

Interested buyers submit standard import/distribution requirements and corporate profiles. We review country-specific import compliance, customs restrictions, and documentation mandates.

02

Specification Mapping & Sourcing

We align on precise technical parameters (e.g. moisture content, grain size, packaging grades). Our agricultural cooperative teams verify availability and lock in volume schedules.

03

Quality Assurance & Lab Audits

Prior to packaging, commodity samples undergo strict laboratory testing for heavy metals, aflatoxins, and pesticide residues. Test results and reports are shared with the buyer.

04

Contract Execution & Letter of Credit

Contracts are signed setting firm delivery schedules, port coordinates, and payment conditions. We establish secure trade channels using bank-verified Letters of Credit (L/C) or advanced T/T.

05

Custom Packaging & Port Dispatch

Products are custom packaged (vacuum sealed, nitrogen flushed) under strict HACCP rules and dispatched. Export documents are compiled and forwarded to the buyer for smooth clearance.

Partnership FAQs

Our typical MOQ is one Full Container Load (FCL), equivalent to 20 to 25 Metric Tons depending on the commodity. For specialty products like Vetiver Essential Oil, we accommodate smaller initial air-freight consignments starting from 100 kg to support market validation trials before scaling.

Yes. We offer complete OEM and Private Label sourcing. Our facilities support custom bag dimensions (from 1 kg retail pouches to 50 kg bulk sacks), multi-color printing, non-woven fabric bags, and modified atmosphere packaging (nitrogen flushing/vacuum sealing) to maintain maximum freshness on foreign retail shelves.

We accept 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight issued by prime international banks, or structured Telegraphic Transfer (T/T) with a 30% advance deposit and 70% against faxed Bill of Lading (B/L) copies. For strategic long-term contract buyers, we can evaluate custom revolving credit limits.

We deploy standard 4-Stage inspection frameworks. All crops are monitored from pre-harvest state, graded using laser sorters, and tested in NABL-accredited labs. Additionally, every dispatch is sealed only after passing final pre-shipment inspections from leading international agencies like SGS or Bureau Veritas.
