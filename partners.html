Workflow Integration

Our Partner Onboarding Journey

01

Initial Consultation & Compliance Review

Interested foreign buyers looking to purchase Indian exports and international suppliers seeking to enter the Indian market submit their requirements and compliance profiles. We review customs, tariff, and documentation protocols for both import and export lanes.

02

Specification Mapping & Sourcing

We map precise technical parameters (e.g. moisture content, grain size, packaging grades for exports; purity, safety data sheets, and industrial certifications for imports). We verify sourcing channels from local farms/manufacturers or vet overseas supplier facilities.

03

Quality Control & Lab Audits

Commodity and material samples undergo lab testing (SGS/Bureau Veritas) to match regulatory standards. For imports, we confirm Indian customs compliance (FSSAI, CDSCO, AQCS, etc.) and verify destination market requirements for exports.

04

Contract Execution & Trade Financing

Firm delivery and payment agreements are locked in. Together with our bank partners, we execute transactions under Letters of Credit (L/C) or advanced T/T, ensuring complete payment security for both international buyers and supplier divisions.

05

Logistics, Customs Clearance & Dispatch

Export cargoes are custom packaged and dispatched. For import sourcing, our team handles customs clearance, local port logistics, and warehouse distribution to domestic Indian buyers.

Partnership FAQs

For foreign buyers sourcing exports from India, our typical MOQ is one Full Container Load (FCL), equivalent to 20 to 25 Metric Tons depending on the commodity. For specialty products like Vetiver Essential Oil, we accommodate smaller initial air-freight consignments starting from 100 kg. For international suppliers importing and distributing materials into the Indian domestic market, we evaluate trial shipment volumes on a case-by-case basis to align with local regulatory clearance pathways.

Yes. We offer complete OEM and Private Label sourcing. Our facilities support custom bag dimensions (from 1 kg retail pouches to 50 kg bulk sacks), multi-color printing, non-woven fabric bags, and modified atmosphere packaging (nitrogen flushing/vacuum sealing) to maintain maximum freshness on foreign retail shelves.

We accept 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight issued by prime international banks, or structured Telegraphic Transfer (T/T) with a 30% advance deposit and 70% against faxed Bill of Lading (B/L) copies. For strategic long-term contract buyers, we can evaluate custom revolving credit limits.

We deploy standard 4-Stage inspection frameworks. All crops are monitored from pre-harvest state, graded using laser sorters, and tested in NABL-accredited labs. Additionally, every dispatch is sealed only after passing final pre-shipment inspections from leading international agencies like SGS or Bureau Veritas.

For foreign buyers looking to purchase Indian exports, we require business registration, destination import license, and target specs to initiate quotes. For international suppliers looking to enter the Indian market, we facilitate local regulatory approvals, customs clearance, and connect you with domestic manufacturing or distribution channels. Both pathways undergo standard KYC and compliance onboarding.
